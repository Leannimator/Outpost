# Privacy Notice (template)

> A starting point, not legal advice. Edit it so it's true for your shop, and check the rules where you and your customers live (for example GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA in California).

**[Your shop name]** collects only what it needs to sell you something.

## What I collect
- **Orders:** your name, email, shipping address and what you bought.
- **Auctions:** the name and email you give when you join to bid, and your bids. If the shop asks for a card first, Stripe keeps the card and I only see the brand and last four digits.
- **Reviews:** your review, and your first name plus last initial.
- **Security:** your IP address is used briefly to stop spam and abuse.

## What I don't do
I don't sell your information. I don't run advertising trackers. Outpost does not send marketing emails by itself.

## Who else sees it
- **Stripe** processes payments (see stripe.com/privacy).
- **Cloudflare Turnstile**, if turned on, checks that you're a person.
- **My hosting company** stores the shop's data.
- **Shipping carriers** get your name and address to deliver your order.

## How long I keep it
Order records are kept as long as I need them for taxes and bookkeeping. Ask me to remove your bidder account any time.

## Your choices
Email me at [your email] to see, correct or delete your information, unless I must keep it by law.

*Last updated: [date]*
